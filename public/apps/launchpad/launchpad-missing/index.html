01

Still on 15? Restore the built-in Launchpad first.

macOS 15 still includes Launchpad. If only its Dock icon is missing, you usually do not need an alternative app.

  1. Open Finder and choose Applications in the sidebar.
  2. Find Launchpad and double-click it to make sure it opens.
  3. Drag Launchpad back to the app area of the Dock.

You can also search for “Launchpad” with Spotlight. If it is still missing, first confirm that your Mac is running macOS 15.

02

Just upgraded? Try the built-in options first.

The app entry points changed in macOS 26 / 27. Your apps did not disappear with Launchpad—the way you open and browse them changed.

Spotlight
Press Command + Space, type an app name and press Return. Best when you already know which app you want.
Apps
Open Apps from the Dock to browse the app list provided by macOS.
Dock folder
Drag the Applications folder from Finder to the folder area of the Dock, then choose Grid as its display style.

These methods open apps, but they do not restore the classic Launchpad’s manual pages, folders or icon positions. If you find apps by location, take a look at LaunchOS.

macOS 27 did not restore the classic Launchpad.

If you are staying on macOS 26 and waiting for 27 to bring Launchpad back: macOS 27 does not restore the classic Launchpad, its old folders or manual pages.

You can keep using Apps, Spotlight and the Dock, or use an independent app such as LaunchOS to restore the familiar visual browsing experience in one step.
