Upgrade Your LaunchOS License

Enter your existing license details to verify your eligibility and explore special upgrade offers that let you add more Macs at a discounted price.

Before You Upgrade

A few things to know before upgrading your license:

  • You can upgrade your license up to a limit of 5 Macs. To use LaunchOS on more than 5 Macs, purchase an additional license.
  • Upgrading costs less than buying additional licenses. Processing fees and taxes can make it slightly more expensive than buying a larger plan upfront.
