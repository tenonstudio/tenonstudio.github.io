Find Your License

Forgot your LaunchOS license? Enter the email address you used to purchase or activate LaunchOS, and we’ll resend the licenses associated with it.

Having trouble receiving the email?

If you’re unable to receive your license email, for example:

  • You entered the wrong email address when purchasing Change Email
  • You no longer remember which email address you used
  • You no longer have access to the email address

Please contact us at our official email and include your invoice, payment record, or any other information that can help us verify your purchase.
