Launchpad 0.3.9

  • Window-mode keyboard selection now uses a highlight matched to the app icon's footprint and corner shape.
  • Live page displacement moved out of the catalog into a dedicated PageDragState; 60–120 Hz swipes no longer invalidate the whole launcher.
  • Adjacent pages are pre-rendered and composited, so page swipes in window mode are smoother and less likely to hitch.
