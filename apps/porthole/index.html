02 / Window mirroring窗口镜像

Porthole

Any window. Always in sight. 任意窗口，始终在视线里。

Porthole turns any app window or screen region into a real-time floating mirror. Keep a chart, a chat, a video, or a reference above your work without switching spaces. Porthole 把任意应用窗口或屏幕区域变成实时悬浮镜像。行情、聊天、视频或参考资料可以一直浮在工作上方，不用来回切换桌面。

What it does它能做什么

Keep the important thing in view.把重要的东西留在视线里。

Porthole mirrors real rendered pixels, so the floating window looks and behaves like the source instead of a reconstructed copy.Porthole 镜像的是真实渲染画面，悬浮窗看起来、动起来都和来源一致，不是重新拼出来的网页副本。

01

Window or region窗口或区域

Mirror a whole app window, or drag-select any region inside it, including a circular selection.镜像整个应用窗口，或拖拽截取窗口内任意区域，也支持圆形选区。

02

Three display modes三种展示方式

One window per mirror, grouped by app, or several sources in one unified container.每个镜像独立成窗、按 App 分组，或多个来源共用一个统一容器。

03

Click-through点击穿透

Adjust opacity and let clicks pass through a mirror so it never blocks the work underneath.调整透明度，让点击穿透镜像，不会挡住下面的工作。

04

Scroll to zoom滚轮缩放

Hover and scroll to enlarge a mirror while keeping the live source crisp.悬停滚动即可放大镜像，同时保持实时来源清晰。

05

Menu bar pin驻留菜单栏

Pin a live thumbnail to the menu bar and restore the full mirror with one click.把实时缩略图留在菜单栏，一键恢复完整镜像。

06

Private by design本地优先

Frames are processed on your Mac. No frames are uploaded or stored.所有画面都在本机处理，不上传、不存储画面数据。

Platform平台

macOS 13+Built for Apple silicon Macs.为 Apple 芯片 Mac 设计。

Permission权限

Screen recording屏幕录制macOS asks once; restart Porthole after granting access.macOS 会询问一次，授权后请重启 Porthole。

Price价格

Free免费Every feature, no account, no license key.全部功能免费，无需账号或激活码。

Use it for适合这些场景

A second view, without a second screen.不接第二块屏，也能多一个视野。

Keep the thing you are monitoring next to the thing you are doing. Porthole is for information that needs to stay visible, not another window to manage.把要盯的东西放在正在做的事旁边。Porthole 给的是需要一直可见的信息，而不是又一个要管理的窗口。

Watch a chart while writing. Keep a call or a livestream in the corner while taking notes. Compare two pages without splitting the screen.写东西时盯行情，开会或看直播时把画面放在角落，做笔记时对比两个页面而不用分屏。

Pin it above other apps, make it semi-transparent, or let clicks pass through. The window stays useful without taking over.置顶、半透明、点击穿透。它一直在，但不会接管你的桌面。
