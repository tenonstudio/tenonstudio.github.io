Privacy Policy隐私政策

Flip is designed to keep your content on your Mac.Flip 的设计目标是让你的内容留在本机。

What is stored存储的内容

Preferences, wallpaper or mirror settings, local scripts, and license state are stored on this Mac. We do not provide accounts or cloud sync.偏好设置、壁纸或镜像设置、本地脚本以及授权状态都保存在这台 Mac 上。我们不提供账号或云同步。

Screen and wallpaper content屏幕与壁纸内容

Settings, local scripts, and control state stay local. We do not upload automation results, Bluetooth information, or files.设置、本地脚本和控制状态都保存在本机。我们不会上传自动化结果、蓝牙信息或文件。

Network access网络访问

Flip itself does not send analytics. Controls that use macOS automation, Bluetooth, or location do so only when you activate them; purchase and license activation use Lemon Squeezy.Flip 本身不发送分析数据。使用 macOS 自动化、蓝牙或位置的控制项只会在你主动启用时访问相应系统能力；购买和授权激活使用 Lemon Squeezy。

Purchases and licensing购买与授权

License activation and purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy. Payment details and billing information are handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy; we receive the license information needed to activate the app.授权激活和购买由 Lemon Squeezy 处理。支付与账单信息由 Lemon Squeezy 按其隐私政策处理；我们只接收激活 App 所需的授权信息。

Analytics分析数据

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.App 不包含第三方分析或广告 SDK。

Contact联系

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Tenon Studio website.如对隐私政策有疑问，可通过 Tenon Studio 网站联系。
