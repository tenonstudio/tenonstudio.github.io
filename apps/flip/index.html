01 / Menu bar utility菜单栏效率工具

Flip

Every switch you reach for, in one quiet panel. 每天要用的 macOS 开关，收进一个安静的面板。

Flip lives in your menu bar and gathers the system toggles, shortcuts, and small scripts you use every day. Open it with a click or a global shortcut, move with the arrow keys, and press Return or Space to act. Flip 常驻菜单栏，把每天要用的系统开关、快捷键和小脚本收在一起。点开图标或按全局快捷键打开，方向键选择，回车或空格执行。

What it does它能做什么

A smaller control room for the Mac.一个更小的 Mac 控制台。

Flip is not a dashboard. It is a short list of the things you actually change during a day, with a consistent switch for each one.Flip 不是一块大而全的仪表盘，而是一份你每天真的会动的开关清单。每个开关都用一致的方式工作。

01

33 controls33 个控制项

Low Power, Dark Mode, Keep Awake, network, Bluetooth, AirDrop, cleanup, display sleep, and more.低电量、深色模式、保持唤醒、网络、蓝牙、隔空投送、清理、显示器休眠等。

02

Keyboard-first键盘优先

Record a shortcut to open the panel. Use the arrow keys to select, Return or Space to act.录制打开面板的全局快捷键。方向键选择，回车或空格执行。

03

Custom commands自定义命令

Add one-shot actions, on/off scripts, or preset choices with a single .js file.用一个 .js 文件加入一次性动作、开关脚本或预设选项。

04

Your order你的顺序

Choose what appears in the panel and drag the list into the order that matches your day.选择面板里出现什么，再拖动成符合你工作节奏的顺序。

05

System Helper系统 Helper

Privileged controls such as Low Power and clamshell sleep use a separate System Helper.低电量、合盖不休眠等需要特权的控制由独立的 System Helper 完成。

06

No account无需账号

Your configuration and scripts stay on your Mac. No account is needed to use Flip.配置和脚本保存在本机，使用 Flip 不需要注册账号。

Privacy隐私

Local by default默认本地Settings and scripts are stored on your Mac. No account or cloud sync.设置和脚本保存在本机，不需要账号或云同步。

A day with Flip用 Flip 的一天

Small actions, no window hunting.小动作，不用到处找窗口。

Before a call, mute the mic and hide the desktop. During deep work, keep the Mac awake and turn off distractions. At the end of the day, clean up and sleep the display.开会前静音麦克风、隐藏桌面；专注时保持唤醒、关掉干扰；下班前清理并让显示器休眠。

Flip should feel like a physical switch panel: predictable, quick, and a little quieter than the system settings it replaces.Flip 应该像一块实体开关面板：可预期、快，而且比系统设置安静一点。

The panel is designed for both the mouse and the keyboard. Hover or use the arrow keys; the highlighted row is always the one that will run.面板同时照顾鼠标和键盘。悬停或按方向键，当前高亮的那一行永远就是将要执行的那一行。

If something needs a shell script, use a custom command. If it needs a privileged system change, Flip asks the System Helper instead of running everything as root.需要脚本的事情交给自定义命令；需要特权的系统改动交给 System Helper，而不是把所有东西都以 root 运行。
